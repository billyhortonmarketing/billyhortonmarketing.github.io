I'm based in Princeton, Texas, and my marketing work includes social media, email newsletters, SEO-focused writing, and audience research. Across these channels, I focus on developing content that is relevant, clear, and useful to its intended audience.

Before focusing on marketing, I spent years coordinating work in technical environments. That taught me the value of asking good questions, staying organized, communicating clearly, and following through.

At PMI DFW Properties, I've created content for two very different audiences: renters looking for practical advice and property owners making investment decisions. With Outdoor Weekend Warriors, I apply that same audience-first thinking to outdoor education and weekend adventures.

I'm finishing my B.S. in Marketing at the University of Texas at Dallas, with graduation expected in December 2026. I'm pursuing opportunities in digital marketing that bring together research, strategy, writing, and creative problem-solving.
