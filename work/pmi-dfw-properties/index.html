THE CONTEXT

Start with
the people.

Summer in North Texas brings real maintenance pressure for rental properties, especially around cooling systems and the cost of deferred upkeep. For property owners, helpful seasonal guidance is more valuable than another generic service promotion.

At PMI DFW Properties, I helped develop a July 2026 owner newsletter called 'Protect Your Rental Investment During Peak Summer.' The goal was to make preventive action approachable while reinforcing PMI's role as a knowledgeable property-management resource. This was one campaign within my broader work across social media and email.

MY APPROACH

From strategy
to execution.

My contributions and the process behind this project.

01

Lead with the owner's concern

Framed the campaign around protecting property value, reducing preventable surprises, and making smart maintenance decisions during peak summer heat—topics with immediate relevance for DFW rental owners.

02

Make the inbox message useful

Developed the subject line '☀️ Protect Your Rental Investment During Peak Summer' with the preview 'Practical strategies to protect your property, preserve long-term value, and avoid costly surprises.' The tone balanced professional expertise with clear, useful advice.

03

Translate advice into a visual takeaway

Helped develop a 'Summer Property Protection Checklist' infographic for the newsletter and guided its presentation toward a wider format, clearer layout, and a more restrained use of orange. The infographic was included in the published send.

04

Review the published results

The July newsletter was sent and reported a 20.4% open rate and 2% click rate. Those figures are specific to this campaign; they are not presented as an overall email average or evidence of year-over-year growth.
