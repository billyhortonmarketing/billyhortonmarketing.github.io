THE CONTEXT

Start with
the people.

Outdoor inspiration often focuses on dramatic destinations and expensive gear. For people balancing work, family, and everything else, the bigger challenge can be finding an outing that fits the time and experience they actually have.

I created Outdoor Weekend Warriors to make that first step easier. The brand began with close-to-home adventures in North Texas and uses practical guides, accessible advice, and nature education to encourage people to make more of their weekends. Its tagline is 'Your weekend. Your Adventure.'

MY APPROACH

From strategy
to execution.

My contributions and the process behind this project.

01

Start with the barrier

Build for people who are interested in the outdoors but short on time, uncertain about where to begin, or reluctant to commit to a complicated trip.

02

Give the brand a voice

Keep the writing informative and approachable, with a little humor where it feels natural. The reader should feel welcome without being talked down to.

03

Match topics to real questions

Plan different entry points: a local trail someone can find through search, an outdoor topic they want explained, or an easy activity to do with family.

04

Turn ideas into usable articles

Write with descriptive headings, practical details, and clear next steps. Use those longer pieces as a foundation for an ongoing editorial and social-content plan.

THE EDITORIAL STRATEGY

Different topics.
One clear audience.

The strategy focuses on helping busy people discover approachable outdoor experiences. That audience need shapes three content pillars, from nearby destinations to practical activities.

01

Discover nearby

Help people choose a realistic place to go, with enough detail to plan the outing.

02

Learn something useful

Explain outdoor ideas in plain language and connect them to everyday life.

03

Make it achievable

Offer low-pressure activities that work with family schedules, budgets, and experience levels.
